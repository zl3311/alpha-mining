# fnd6_drlt

Deferred Revenue, Long-Term. Captures companies with prepaid contracts (SaaS, subscriptions, defense).

## Mechanism

High deferred revenue relative to price signals locked-in future revenue the market undervalues. Companies with large backlogs of prepaid contracts have predictable future cash flows that reduce earnings risk, but the market prices them like other firms with similar current earnings.
