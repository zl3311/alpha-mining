# cogs

Cost of Goods Sold normalized by price. Captures capital-intensive firms trading cheaply.

## Mechanism

High COGS/price = revenue-heavy firm trading at a discount relative to its operating scale. Essentially a price-to-sales variant weighted by cost structure. Works because market overpenalizes high-cost firms during temporary margin compression.
