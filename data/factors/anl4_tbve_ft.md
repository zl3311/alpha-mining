# anl4_tbve_ft

Tangible book value per share estimate (fiscal year target)

## Mechanism

Analyst estimate of future tangible book value. Higher estimated TBVE signals analysts expect the firm to accumulate tangible assets, which is bullish for asset-heavy sectors. Related to book value revision family (H-002) but captures level rather than revision direction.
