# MARKET Neutralization for Decorrelation

Switching from SUBINDUSTRY to MARKET neut reduces self-corr by 0.10-0.20.
Key use case: when a signal family is BLOCKED by self-corr under SUBINDUSTRY.
itci+acdo MARKET: self-corr 0.492 (lowest ever found).
ptp MARKET: self-corr 0.559.
