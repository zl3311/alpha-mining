```
ts_decay_linear(rank(fnd6_itci / close) + zscore(ts_mean(implied_volatility_call_60 - implied_volatility_put_60, 44)) + rank(fnd6_drlt / close) + rank(fnd6_acdo / close), 5)
```

## Mechanism

4-factor additive blend combining signals from two independent data sources:

1. **rank(fnd6_itci / close)** — Inventory/total capital investment relative to price.
   High values indicate capital-intensive firms with large inventory positions relative
   to market cap. Captures balance sheet quality and supply chain signaling.

2. **zscore(ts_mean(IV60_call - IV60_put, 44))** — 44-day smoothed cross-sectional
   z-score of 60-day implied volatility call-put skew. Captures options market
   directional sentiment — positive skew means calls are relatively expensive vs puts,
   indicating bullish options flow.

3. **rank(fnd6_drlt / close)** — Deferred revenue/total liabilities relative to price.
   High deferred revenue signals pre-paid future obligations (subscription revenue,
   advance payments), indicating revenue quality and visibility.

4. **rank(fnd6_acdo / close)** — Accumulated depreciation relative to price.
   High accumulated depreciation relative to market cap indicates established,
   capital-intensive firms with substantial fixed assets — a value/maturity signal.

## Why It Works

The cross-dataset combination exploits two orthogonal market inefficiencies:
- **Fundamental6 (50% coverage, quarterly updates)**: Slow-moving balance sheet
  quality signals that the market underprices due to processing complexity
- **Option8 IV60 (97% coverage, daily updates)**: Fast-moving options sentiment
  that captures informed trading activity before price impact

The structural independence between datasets creates natural decorrelation from
existing book entries (max self-corr 0.66), while the complementary time scales
(quarterly fundamentals + daily options) capture both persistent value and
short-term sentiment dimensions simultaneously.
